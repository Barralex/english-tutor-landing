# TeroTalk

**IB-trained English teacher · Kids at home, business English online · Ciudad de la Costa, Uruguay**

One brand over two businesses: in-home lessons for kids across Ciudad de la Costa, and a six-hour online program for working professionals. Three pages, and a home that sends each visitor to the one that is theirs.

***

Flor teaches; TeroTalk is only the name over the door, and the site was built so the name never gets in front of the person. The two services stay apart on purpose, because the parent who is tired of driving a kid across town and the professional who freezes in an English meeting are not the same person and cannot be told the same thing. Nothing here asks for a form or a password. Every path ends where the work actually starts: a message to Flor.

***

Three static pages. HTML and three stylesheets; no build step, no dependencies, nothing to install. Every push to `main` is checked before it ships, and a check that fails leaves the live site exactly as it was.

Published at [terotalk.com](https://terotalk.com/) on GitHub Pages.
